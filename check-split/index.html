Check Split

An Android bill-splitting tool for dividing a restaurant total more fairly when people ordered different amounts.

Get it on Google PlaySee all apps

What it does

  • Split restaurant bills proportionally.
  • Include tax in the final calculation.
  • Account for flat fees and shared charges.
  • Use custom tips instead of relying on a one-size-fits-all split.
Useful for: Useful for dinners with friends, group meals, mixed spending amounts and any receipt where tax, tip or shared charges make the math annoying.

Simple by design

This app is built around one practical job instead of trying to become an all-in-one platform. The goal is to make the core task easy to understand and quick to use.
