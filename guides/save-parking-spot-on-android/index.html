How to Save Your Parking Spot on Android

The easiest time to save your parking spot is the moment you step out of the car. A few seconds of setup can save a lot of wandering later.

A simple parking workflow

  1. Before walking away, save your current car location.
  2. Add a short note if GPS alone will not tell the whole story.
  3. In a garage, record the level, row, section or landmark.
  4. If useful, take a quick photo of a nearby sign or pillar.
  5. When you return, open the saved location and use walking directions as a guide.

When location alone is not enough

Dense parking garages, airports and large event lots can be difficult because multiple levels may share similar coordinates. A text note or photo adds the human detail that a map pin cannot always capture.

Make the habit automatic

Tie the action to something you already do: lock the car, save the spot, then walk away. The less friction the process has, the more likely you are to remember it when you actually need it.
