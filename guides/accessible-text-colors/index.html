How to Choose Accessible Text Colors

Start with the actual foreground and background colors you plan to use, check their contrast ratio, then adjust the combination instead of relying only on what looks readable on your own screen.

A practical workflow

  1. Choose the exact text and background colors you plan to use.
  2. Enter or sample both colors in a contrast checker.
  3. Check the ratio against the WCAG level and text size you are targeting.
  4. If the pair fails, change the foreground, background or both.
  5. Recheck the final values after design changes.

Do not forget states and small UI text

Check more than body copy. Navigation labels, buttons, form hints, disabled-looking states and text placed over images can all end up with different contrast than the main design system.
