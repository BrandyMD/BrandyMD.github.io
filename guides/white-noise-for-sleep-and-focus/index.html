White Noise for Sleep and Focus: A Practical Setup

Some people prefer a steady, predictable background sound because it can make sudden environmental noises feel less noticeable. The best setup is usually the one that stays comfortable and unobtrusive.

Pick a steady sound

Fan-like noise works well for many people because it is continuous and does not demand attention. Avoid tracks with obvious loops, voices or dramatic changes if your goal is a neutral background.

Keep the volume comfortable

The sound should sit in the background rather than dominate the room. Start lower than you think you need and increase only if the surrounding environment is still distracting.

For sleep

  • Use a consistent sound you can leave running without interaction.
  • Keep your phone positioned safely and avoid uncomfortable volume levels.
  • Pair the sound with the rest of your normal bedtime routine rather than treating it as a cure-all.

For focus

  • Use the same background sound during work or study sessions to reduce novelty.
  • Keep notifications and other audio interruptions off when possible.
  • If the sound itself becomes distracting, lower it or switch it off.
