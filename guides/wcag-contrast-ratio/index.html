What Does a 4.5:1 Contrast Ratio Mean?

A contrast ratio compares the relative luminance of two colors. For normal-sized text, WCAG 2.1 Level AA commonly uses a minimum ratio of 4.5:1.

Common text thresholds

  • AA normal text: 4.5:1
  • AA large text: 3:1
  • AAA normal text: 7:1
  • AAA large text: 4.5:1

What that means in practice

If a foreground text color and its background fall below the applicable threshold, adjust one or both colors and test again. Visual judgment alone is not enough because screens, lighting and individual vision differ.

Contrast is one accessibility check, not the whole checklist

Good contrast improves readability, but accessible design also includes things like semantic structure, keyboard access, clear focus states, labels and meaningful text alternatives.
