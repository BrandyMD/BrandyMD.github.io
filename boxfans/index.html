A simple Android box-fan sound app for steady background noise without extra complexity.

What it does

  • Play box-fan-style white noise from your Android phone.
  • Use steady background sound for sleep, focus or relaxation.
  • Keep the experience simple and purpose-built around fan noise.
Useful for: Useful for bedtime routines, quiet work sessions, study time, travel and masking distracting background sounds.

Simple by design

This app is built around one practical job instead of trying to become an all-in-one platform. The goal is to make the core task easy to understand and quick to use.
