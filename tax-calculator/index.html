Tax Calculator – Sales Tax

A simple Android sales-tax calculator for turning a price and tax rate into the final amount.

Get it on Google PlaySee all apps

What it does

  • Enter an item price.
  • Enter the applicable tax rate.
  • Instantly calculate the sales-tax amount.
  • See the final total after tax.
Useful for: Useful for shopping, budgeting, estimates and quick checkout math.

Simple by design

This app is built around one practical job instead of trying to become an all-in-one platform. The goal is to make the core task easy to understand and quick to use.
