Contrast Checker & screenshot hex color picker

A practical Android tool for sampling exact colors from screenshots and checking whether foreground/background combinations meet common WCAG 2.1 contrast thresholds.

Get it on Google Play

What you can do

  • Upload a screenshot, artwork or image and sample an exact pixel color.
  • Extract and copy six-digit hex color values.
  • Compare foreground and background colors with an instant contrast ratio.
  • See pass/fail guidance for AA and AAA text contrast thresholds.
  • Swap colors, enter hex values manually, and preview combinations.
Useful for: web design, UI/UX, graphic design, app mockups, brand palettes, accessibility reviews and grabbing colors from inspiration screenshots.
