Spot Saver – Save Parking Spot

A simple Android parking helper for saving where you left the car before you walk away.

Get it on Google PlaySee all apps

What it does

  • Save your parked car location with one tap.
  • Add garage notes when a map pin is not enough.
  • Keep offline photos of signs, rows or nearby landmarks.
  • Open walking directions back to your saved vehicle location.
Useful for: Useful for large parking lots, parking garages, airports, events, shopping centers and unfamiliar areas.

Simple by design

This app is built around one practical job instead of trying to become an all-in-one platform. The goal is to make the core task easy to understand and quick to use.
